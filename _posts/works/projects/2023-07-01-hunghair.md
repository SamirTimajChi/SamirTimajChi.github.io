## Concept

*Hung Hair* delves into the often overlooked executions led by the standing and battling for Women's Rights — as well as the shooting of people on the streets — and all about fighting for the freedom of dress, a right suppressed by compulsory rules imposed by governments in countries such as the Islamic Republic of Afghanistan and Iran, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia, Chechnya, and others.

The work relates to the exploitative point of view of the human from government, applied to part or all of society. What is happening in these lands has nothing to do with hypothetical texts on papers — it is a loud yell and a bloody fight.

## Structure

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/hunghair/hung_hair.png" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true caption="Performance setup — performer hung from feet above a prepared canvas" %}

1. The performer is hung from their feet.
2. On the floor a canvas is placed, under which several piezoelectric sensors (microphones) are mounted to capture data that will be manipulated and transformed into sounds.
3. A washable bucket of paint the same color as the canvas — preferably canvas, costume, makeup, and painting all in white.
4. Dramaturgy of lights.

---

## How to Perform

The hanging performer will start painting with their hair as a paintbrush. At the end, they cut their hair and spread it on the canvas, then imitate the electronic sounds created by touching the canvas.
