## Intro

This episodic statement explores the territory of concept and structure, reflecting an artist's recognition of crisis from diverse perspectives. Having lived as a minority in Iran, later as an immigrant in Russia and Germany, and through encounters with various people, the artist aims to achieve a broad, universal observation. By empathizing and putting themselves in others' shoes, the artist seeks to understand the challenges of different pathways, thereby gaining deeper meaning through alternative viewpoints. Through this process, the following question becomes recognized as a unified point to share with others.

### What is the Current Crisis?

Skipping a long list of what can be defined as a crisis and picking only one topic, freedom is the most likely choice. The answer to the question 'Why?' could be because everything starts from the point where we assume a boundary for the 'individual', both in terms of hypothesis and reality.

The limitation of freedom applies to every individual, with extreme cases often related to nationality, gender, race, sex, and other factors.

Earlier, I mentioned a portion of the limitations on freedom that affect everyone. While extreme cases might not be the only target, these limitations still impact already persecuted minorities. These limitations can lead to forced persecutions that threaten the very existence of a large number of individuals.

As a result of these limitations, identity crisis has become a topic frequently used for various purposes, such as in social media, politics, philosophy, art, science, and other criteria of criticism. Dealing with identity crises as individuals not only leads to a loss of identity but also results in forgetting the conversation with oneself. In society, this mostly appears as manipulated voices and remains as a blind imitation of given labels coming from immoral and outdated sources.

From the first instance that the human voice comes into existence, an additional instrument can be directly mounted on the larynx to manipulate the sound of speech from scratch. The exploration began with the discovery of the first version during an extended sound rehearsal session on the piano with Lydia Krifka-Dobes in Fall 2022.

I was thinking aloud when, accidentally, an electric massager made contact with my throat. The resulting sound was so interesting that we couldn't resist listening to it a second time. This experience created a need to explore the combination of the human voice and transducer speakers on the throat in multiple vocal pieces of music, and that exploration is still in progress.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/tou/transducer-worn.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true caption="Transducer worn at the throat during performance" %}

### Technical Approach

When there are two sounds from two different sources with slightly different frequencies, they create a phenomenon known in physics as beats. This special kind of beating, which comes through frequency interference, explains how most birds can produce specific vibratos thanks to their unique throat and larynx anatomy. This phenomenon is also a specific vocal technique in Persian and Azeri music called *Tahrir*, where the singer imitates the same sound by phases of blocking the vocal cords to create a falsetto appoggiatura in different intervals before the main tone.

In this artistic approach, a vocal ensemble with four singers experienced the same phenomenon by singing while a transducer, which plays a sine wave with a slight pitch difference, made contact with their throats.

**In the Score:**
- Symbols are accurately chosen from meaningful musical symbols and IPA characters.
- Green lines and symbols represent notes for vocalists.
- Pink lines represent sine waves produced by transducers.

**Equipment:** Wearable transducers (such as a necklace) should directly touch the bones of the throat. A slight pitch difference (a few Hertz) will create physical beating, which resonates in the mouth of the singer.

Equipment was provided with the generous support of the **Aksenov Family Foundation** for the NOMADISM project at The VOICES Performing Arts Festival, November 16, 2024, RadialSystem Berlin. Premiered by **PHØNIX16**.

**Electronics:** A Half-Dom ambisonic system is required to play the electronic part. The venue was equipped by L-ACOUSTICS using L-ISA software for spatialization.

### Choreography

An abstract choreography for one dancer, who moves from one side of the stage and disappears on the other side. Three levels of speed apply:

- **Fastest:** Head movement — looking at the audience, then looking away.
- **Medium:** Leg movement — incorporating backward movement.
- **Slow:** Hand movement — starting wide and open, culminating in a self-embrace.
